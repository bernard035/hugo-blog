法4

倒序相加

![图片说明](/images/890624701c0a27c0.png)

```
T=int(input())
mod=1000000007
def f(n):
    return n*(n+1)*(n*2+1)//6
for _ in range(T):
    l,r=map(int,input().split())
    ans=(f(r)-f(l-1))*3+r-l+1
    print(ans%mod)
```
